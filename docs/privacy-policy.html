Chips — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026 · Applies to the Chips mobile app for iOS and Android

Chips is a poker-night balance tracker. It records who bought in for how much, who cashed out for how much, and who owes whom. This policy explains what the app does with that information. The short version: everything stays on your device, and we collect nothing.

What the app stores

  • Player names you type in (first names or nicknames are fine; we never ask for real identities).
  • Session records: dates, optional titles or notes, buy-in and cash-out amounts, manual payments.
  • Your settings: default buy-in, chip denominations.

All of this is saved in a local SQLite database inside the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded, synced, or backed up by us. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include the app's data according to your device settings.

What we collect

Nothing. Chips has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no network requests of its own. We do not receive, see, sell, or share any of your data.

Sharing features

When you tap Share image, the app renders a settlement card and hands it to your phone's standard share sheet. Where it goes from there (Messages, WhatsApp, Photos, etc.) is entirely your choice and governed by that app's policies. Copy to clipboard places plain text on your device clipboard only.

Permissions

Chips does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos. Saving a shared image to your photo library, if you choose that in the share sheet, is handled by the operating system.

Children

Chips is a bookkeeping tool for adults playing home card games. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that change any of the above (for example cloud sync or advertising), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change.
